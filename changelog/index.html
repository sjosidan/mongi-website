What's new in Mongi

A running log of releases across iOS, Android, HarmonyOS, and the web demo. Newest first.

May 29, 2026

Behind-the-scenes tune-up

🍎 iOS 1.0.2 (32) 🤖 Android 1.0.2 (32)
  • Daily streak reminders — get a nudge every evening to play and keep your streak alive. Toggle it in Settings. (Previously these didn't reliably fire — now fixed.)
  • Saved categories self-heal — a category removed in an update no longer lingers as an empty tab; it's cleaned up automatically.
  • External keyboard support — type on a physical keyboard when one's attached to your tablet.
  • Privacy-friendly analytics added so we can see overall activity and improve the games — anonymous and aggregate, no personal data.

May 15, 2026

Target tune-up + sign-in polish + web demo launch

🍎 iOS 1.0.1 🤖 Android 1.0.1+31 📦 HarmonyOS 1.0.2 (1000005) 🌐 Web mongi.app/play
  • Target game: backing out with one number left no longer auto-finishes the game — your progress is saved.
  • Target game: close-but-not-exact submissions now show a confirmation dialog with the partial score you'd lock in. Bullseyes always pay at least 1000 points.
  • Sign-in: a full-screen spinner now appears immediately when you tap Google or Apple, so it's clear something is happening.
  • Sign-in screen: fixed a stray strip of unthemed color at the bottom.
  • Web demo launched at mongi.app/play — no signup, unlimited replays, physical keyboard works for Lingo / Anagram / Crossword / Hangman / Equadle / NumCross / CageCalc.
  • About page: displayed version now matches the actual app version.

May 2026

Categories management + first-launch welcome

🍎 iOS 1.0.0 (30) 🤖 Android 1.0.0 (29-30) 📦 HarmonyOS 1.0.1 (1000004)
  • Welcome modal on first launch explains the three-category structure (Words / Math / Colors) and lets you pick the ones you want to play.
  • Settings → My Categories for adding or removing categories any time.
  • iOS: first App Store submission, iPhone-only.
  • Android: Open Testing launched on Google Play, then promoted to production.

April 13, 2026

Polish + sync fixes

📦 HarmonyOS 1.0.1 (1000003)
  • Cross-device score sync now correctly handles math and colors categories. (Previously they'd silently come back as English after a login sync.)
  • Friends head-to-head now spans every category you both play, with a small flag badge to keep the same game in different categories distinct.
  • Stats All Time now combines daily + unlimited (was unlimited-only).
  • CageCalc: Reset has a confirmation dialog, conflict detection is more accurate.
  • Cipher / Nixnum / Target / Crossword: timer starts on first input rather than on page load.
  • Volley: win screen shows ball positions after the winning move; view mode replays full round history.
  • Mosaic: validation redesigned with clearer 3-state feedback.

April 7, 2026

Mongi launches on HarmonyOS

📦 HarmonyOS 1.0.0 (1000001)
  • Day one. Mongi is live on Huawei AppGallery.
  • 12 games across English, Math, and Colors categories.
  • 10 themes — five clean, five immersive with animated ambient effects.
  • Mongoose avatar builder — 14-layer SVG composition, 138 million possible combinations.
  • Streaks + rest days for protecting your run when life happens.
  • Friends, leaderboards, and weekly leagues for the competitive side.
