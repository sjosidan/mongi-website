Delete your Mongi account

Mongi is made by Jade Circuit OÜ in Estonia. This page explains how to delete your Mongi account and all data we hold about you — either yourself in-app, or by emailing us if you can't access the app anymore.

What gets deleted

When you delete your Mongi account, the following is permanently removed from our servers, with no recovery possible:

  • Your display name and friend code
  • Your avatar configuration
  • All your game scores and daily aggregates
  • Your streak history and rest-day inventory
  • All your friendships and pending friend requests
  • Your league placements and weekly history
  • Any push-notification tokens registered for streak reminders
  • Your Firebase Authentication record tied to your account
This is irreversible. Deletion happens immediately. There is no recovery window — your account, scores, streaks, and friend list cannot be restored.

How to delete — Option 1: from inside the app (recommended)

This is the fastest and most reliable way:

  1. Open the Mongi app on your phone.
  2. Tap Settings (the gear icon, bottom-right tab).
  3. Tap Profile.
  4. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm in the dialog.

The account, scores, streaks, friendships, and league record are removed from our servers within seconds. The app signs you out automatically.

How to delete — Option 2: by email request

Use this if you've already lost access to the app or can't sign in:

  1. Email support@jadecircuit.com from the email associated with your Mongi sign-in (Google, Apple, or whichever method you used).
  2. Subject line: Account deletion request.
  3. Include in the message: your display name and friend code (if you remember them) so we can locate the right account.

We will:

  • Verify you control the email address (we may reply asking you to confirm).
  • Delete your data from our servers — typically within 7 business days of verification.
  • Email you back to confirm the deletion is complete.

What doesn't get deleted

For completeness, these are the data categories we either don't store or are required to retain:

  • Local app data on your device — uninstalling the Mongi app removes everything cached locally. Account deletion only covers our servers.
  • Anonymous, aggregated analytics we use to balance the games (e.g. "average completion time for Crossword") — these contain no personal identifiers and are kept indefinitely.
  • Email correspondence with our support address — if you've emailed us, the email itself stays in our inbox under standard email retention; you can ask us to delete those records too in the same request.

Other ways to manage your data

You don't always need to delete your whole account. Lighter options:

  • Edit your profile — change display name, avatar, or friend visibility from Settings → Profile.
  • Disable notifications — Settings → Streak Reminders → off.
  • Sign out without deleting — Settings → Profile → Sign out. Your data stays, you can sign back in later.
  • Export your data — email support@jadecircuit.com with subject Data export request and we'll send a copy of everything we hold about you.
